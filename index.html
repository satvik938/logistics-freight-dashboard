About this dashboard: data, metrics and method
Business question
Are we delivering on time at a reasonable cost, and which carriers and lanes need attention?
Data
~7,600 synthetic shipment records and 480 truck-month fleet records modeled on a regional distributor shipping from Tulsa, OK. Created for this portfolio; not company data.
On-time delivery
Shipments delivered on or before the committed date ÷ all shipments.
Cost per mile
Total freight cost ÷ total loaded miles (includes LTL accessorials).
Fleet utilization
Average share of available truck hours in use across the 40-truck private fleet.
Comparison
Headline figures compare Aug–Oct 2026 with May–Jul 2026.
Method
Python (pandas) to clean and aggregate; KPI logic mirrors SQL group-bys; visualized with D3.

Recommended actions from this view: put Cimarron Transport and Summit Carriers on a 90-day performance plan, shift long-haul Denver and Chicago freight toward intermodal where transit windows allow, and consolidate LTL into FTL on the Dallas and Kansas City lanes.

Portfolio project built with Python (pandas) and D3.js · All data is synthetic. Source code and data are in this repository.
